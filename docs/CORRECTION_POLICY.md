# Correction and Right-of-Reply Policy

As a forensic data engineering project mapping public figures in pandemic governance, we adhere to strict ethical and legal data protection principles:

## 1. Right-of-Reply (Wederhoor)
Prior to publishing any narrative dossier updates or database changes containing potential conflict-of-interest (COI) matrix entries:
1. Affected individuals must be contacted at their institutional email address with the specific claims, evidence, and proposed text.
2. The individual must be given 5 working days to provide a statement or correction.
3. Their response must be published in full, adjacent to the disputed database entry or matrix cell in the dossier and the web application.

## 2. Notice and Takedown (NTD) Procedure
If you identify inaccurate, incomplete, or defamatory data in this repository:
1. Contact the maintainers by opening a GitHub Issue or submitting a request to `privacy@example.com` (SLA address).
2. The maintainers will evaluate the request and apply a temporary `[DISPUTED]` tag within 48 hours.
3. If the disputed claim lacks Level 1 [FACT] evidence, it will be corrected or permanently deleted within 5 working days in compliance with data minimization (GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)) and right to rectification (Art. 16).
